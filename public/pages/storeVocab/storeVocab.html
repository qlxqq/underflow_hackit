Capture words

Collect words from daily life

Snap a sign, box the words you want, or paste some text. Each word becomes a card.

Tips for good photos
  • Photograph one paragraph or half a page at a time.
  • Use good light, hold the phone flat and avoid glare and shadows.
  • Keep the text sharp and fill the frame with it.
  • After the photo, draw a box round just the words you want. Up to 30 words are listed per capture.
  • Want to change something after the words are found? Tap the back arrow to pick different words.
