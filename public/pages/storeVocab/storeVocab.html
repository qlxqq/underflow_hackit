Snap a sign or paste some text. Each word becomes a card.

Tips for good photos
  • Photograph one paragraph or half a page at a time.
  • Use good light, hold the phone flat and avoid glare and shadows.
  • Keep the text sharp and fill the frame with it.
  • Up to 30 words are listed per capture, so a smaller area works best.
  • Want to change something after the words are found? Tap the Capture button at the bottom to go back.
